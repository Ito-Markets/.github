## What we build

Itô Markets brings institutional rigor to prediction markets. We aggregate liquidity across **Polymarket** and **Kalshi** into a unified execution layer with portfolio-level tooling that doesn't exist anywhere else.

**Core infrastructure:**

🔬 **Systematic Index Construction** — Research-driven baskets with transparent methodology and risk factor taxonomy

⚡ **Cross-Exchange Execution** — Unified order routing across prediction market exchanges

📊 **Portfolio Analytics** — Factor models, dynamic rebalancing, and Greeks for prediction market positions

🗂️ **Historical Data** — Comprehensive contract-level data pipelines for backtesting and research
